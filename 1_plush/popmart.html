Pop Mart figurines are one of my favorite things to collect because the designs are always so expressive, colorful, and full of personality. I love browsing different series for inspiration—everything from the tiny details, palettes, and poses makes me excited to create. My collection is still growing, but each one I’ve picked up feels like a tiny piece of joy on my shelf. Here, I’m sharing some of my favorite figures, series that inspire me, and the little things that make PopMart so fun to collect.

───୨Blind Boxes─

Okay with blind boxes, you're gambling. If you only like one design in a series, you're better off buying confirmed boxes online. Ebay has higher prices compared to Mercari. You can also look on depop. However, if you really love an entire series and don't mind which one you get, go for it. My sister and I split 50/50 and we'll buy a case. The last one we got was Nommi Loveliness Never Ends which we got on Amazon. We also trade a lot. I like the moodier themes and she loves the bright colors.
