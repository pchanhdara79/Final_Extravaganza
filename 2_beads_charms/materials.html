For this category, I’ve gathered photos of my personal bead and charm supplies. Most of them are beads, with a few acrylic hangtags mixed in. My collection started small—just crystal beads from Hobby Lobby—and I originally made bracelets only for myself. Once I finally mastered tying secure knots, I began creating pieces for friends and family. I’ve experimented with string, stretchy cord, and different chains—materials I’ll go into more detail about in the next section. For now, here’s a curated set of images to help visualize my inspirations and the materials that shaped my early designs.

Since last year, I've also sourced some hand painted beads. They're chunky, almost candy-like. These beads are painted with colored resin which has a higher viscosity and varying pigments. It's not an easy medium to work with since resin is tricky enough as it is and small detail works can definitely be tedious. Some may offer cheaper prices due to manufacturers being able to produce vast amounts. Others may sell specialized pieces that are pricier. If you compare to decoden, buying from a smaller artist ultimately beats buying a place that produces cookie-cutter copies. Some resin beads utilize gems to make them even fancier -- which costs more than a basic design.

───୨ৎCraft Inspo─

This collection of images represents some of my favorite sources of craft inspiration. I’m really drawn to the colors, palettes, and tiny details—sometimes I’ll spend time browsing PNGs just to clip and arrange them into little digital collages. Premade collages also help me visualize future pieces, whether it’s the vibe, the textures, or the overall aesthetic I want to capture. If you’re interested in creating your own craft projects, I highly recommend exploring bread embroidery, French beading (especially flowers!), and making your own bracelets or necklaces. It’s relaxing, customizable, and genuinely rewarding. And if you look through my gallery of works, you’ll spot some of the beads and elements featured in my earlier collections woven into the final designs.
