My favorite section!! Have you ever seen the videos of people selling scoops? Some people may refer to the bowl as "soup". If you're also familiar with ASMR, you might see creators using visually stimulating setups with sounds overlayed.

Squishies captured that energy perfectly. They offered a soft, squeezable form of nostalgia: think pastel colors, satisfying smushes, and a mix of childhood comfort with intricate designs. Originally, Taba and similar brands set out to give people that familiar joy: something soft, squishy, and visually cute. Over time the range expanded: from food-shaped charms and simple toys to elaborate, art‑style squishy masterpieces.

───୨Types of Beads─

pony, seed, glass, perler, clay, polymer, acrylic charms, pearl beads, alphabet beads, heavy painted beads, color mixes, character beads, etc.

I use a mix of pony beads, transparent sparkly beads, seed beads, pearls, and mini acrylic charms. Each type has a slightly different shine and texture, and mixing them helps build cute dimensional patterns.
