My first serious clay project that involved baking it in a kiln was in middle school. I made a small bowl using leaf impressions and a jar that I carved out my first three initials from. Even though they were simple, I felt like that was one of my first large medium projects outside of painting and drawing. I've also made some small crafts in elementary using air-dry clay. One of my favorite animals is a sheep and that was my first "sculpture". I have no memory of what happened to it, but I also remembered how happy I was. There's always something special about handmade pieces. It has the impressions unique to the creator and not every piece is made the same.

In this section, I will be sharing some of my favorite handmade figure/trinket works that I found.
