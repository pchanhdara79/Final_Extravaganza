Accessories are amazing for elevating a look—they’re small details, but they can completely shift the mood of an outfit. I love how they act like visual accents, adding personality, color, and texture without requiring a full wardrobe change. They can make something simple feel intentional, or make something bold feel cohesive.

While I enjoy wearing jewelry of my own, it’s not always practical for me. I use my hands a lot for crafting, cooking, and day-to-day tasks, so sometimes jewelry can feel distracting or get in the way. And on days when I’m overwhelmed, even small pieces can feel like “too much.” But when my outfits feel like they’re missing something, I’ve found the best alternative way to accessorize is through keychains and bag charms.

I also love adding plush charms or beaded accessories because they give extra texture and a playful flair. The only downside is that light-colored plush charms can stain pretty easily—whether from being outside, rubbing against fabrics, or just everyday use. That’s why I tend to stick with deeper or more vibrant colors; they hold up better and stay looking fresh longer.
