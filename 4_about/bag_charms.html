  • ───୨ৎPurpose─

Bag charms have taken 2025 by storm! Everywhere I look on TikTok and Instagram, people are customizing their bags with little charms, adding personality and flair without changing the bag itself. It’s such a fun and playful way to express your style, and I love how much creativity you can pack into just one tiny accessory. From quirky animals to sparkling gems, the options are endless, and each charm tells a little story. Whether you’re dressing up a simple tote or adding charm to a favorite shoulder bag, these little accessories make everyday bags feel fresh and uniquely yours.

───୨Purses & Bags

Most of my bags are black, which makes them the perfect neutral canvas. Changing out charms lets me shift themes easily without having to buy new bags—it keeps things fun, affordable, and way more sustainable. I’m a big fan of shoulder bags, especially ones with a leather texture. Some of my favorites are from Fossil, Coach, and Michael Kors. I also love a good deal, so TJMaxx and thrifting are where I find a lot of my pieces. Plus, my mom shares her collection with me, which always makes it feel special.
