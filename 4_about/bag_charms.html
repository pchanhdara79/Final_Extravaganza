Hello! This is a playful, interactive space where crafting, collecting, and design come together. Inspired by personal projects and a love for whimsical collectibles, this website is designed as a digital collective that celebrates creativity, storytelling, and the joy of curating.

Here, you will find a carefully curated archive of favorite items—from handmade bag charms and keychains to Popmart plush toys like Labubu, Skullpanda, and Molly, as well as other unique trinkets. Each item is presented with photos, sketches, annotations, and design notes to give an immersive, behind-the-scenes look at the process and inspiration.

Treasure Trove is meant to be more than a gallery; it’s a visual journey that combines scrapbook-style layouts, layered visuals, and playful typography to create a cohesive visual language. Whether you are a collector, designer, or simply curious, you are invited to explore!
