  • ───୨ৎPurpose─

Keychains have become one of my favorite everyday accessories. They’re small, simple, and surprisingly expressive— the kind of item that instantly tells a story about your personality. Whether they’re hanging from your wallet, your phone, or the zipper of your favorite purse, keychains add a little spark of charm to the things you use every day. I especially love finding ones with unique textures, characters, or materials because they make even the most practical items feel fun. They’re also super collectible, and swapping them out keeps everything feeling fresh and personal.

───୨My Keychain Picks

I love mixing materials and themes with my keychains. Some are cute and soft, some are shiny with metal hardware, and some are just fun little characters that make me smile. Many of them come from small shops, thrift finds, or random impulse buys—so each one has a little memory attached. I enjoy rotating them depending on my mood or color palette for the week.

The images below are keychain ideas I'd gravitate towards. This is also like a style grid of I want my keychains to look like. I love dessert themes. If I had more time, I would go in detail about the different keychains but this is a brief blog after all. Feel free to check out the Beads&Charms category for supplies and materials guidance.
