Keychains have always been one of my favorite tiny collectibles. They’re such small items, but they carry a lot of personality—kind of like mini snapshots of the things you love. I’ve noticed that my style changes depending on my mood: sometimes I go for cute characters, sometimes metallic pieces, and other times I love something simple and minimal. They’re an easy way to add a little bling to something as basic as your keys.

I also love how keychains are both functional and decorative. Sometimes I even add a set to my wallets, and it’s the cutest thing ever. My go-tos are pearls, metal beads, and heavy painted beads. My next crafts might include more lucky charms from rope knotting or maybe even a compact mirror made from deco materials.

Please visit the gallery to see some of my works. And if you’re curious about my bead selection, check out the Beads & Charms category—you might find something you love!
