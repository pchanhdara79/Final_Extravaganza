This final project builds upon my Intro to Typography final, where I created a spreadsheet combining typographic skills with graphic design elements. That assignment was quite meticulous, as I learned to work with a grid system in Adobe InDesign. My theme for the project was Y2K—a motif I held onto throughout the class to establish a cohesive theme. For the first project’s bumper sticker, I experimented with a blue and black palette, as well as brown and pink, which influenced my color choices here.

For this website, I wanted to continue exploring and expanding the palette. Since I hadn’t previously experimented much with reds, I let it become a dominant theme. To balance the intensity and add whitespace, I incorporated a soft creme tone instead of stark white, layered with a subtle textured pattern for visual interest and depth. From previous critiques, I've adopted some suggestions and improved. I know that my maximalist tendencies can cause some crowding and clutter, so I made a conscious effort to space out elements and create a more breathable layout.
